Acer Launches New Promotions on Laptops and Gaming Gear

The PC manufacturer is currently providing a variety of discounts across its online storefront, featuring up to 40% off accessory bundles and dedicated pricing for specific buyer groups.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 22, 2026
Updated: September 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Key points

  • Customers can save 40% on select peripherals when building a custom hardware bundle.
  • Verified students, military personnel, and healthcare professionals are eligible for a 15% discount.
  • Weekly rotating deals provide price reductions on various Predator and Nitro series hardware.
  • Extended service plans and antivirus software subscriptions are currently discounted by 10%.

Maximizing Value with Hardware Bundles

Acer has introduced a strategic bundle program on its online storefront designed to help customers save on essential peripherals. When purchasing a desktop PC or a laptop, users are encouraged to utilize the “Build Bundle” feature at checkout. This tool allows shoppers to pair their main computer with accessories such as mice, laptop bags, or headsets at a 40% discount. By bundling up to five products, customers can optimize their savings on a complete setup.

A primary example of these savings is the Nitro Mechanical Keyboard, which sees its price reduced from $50 to $30 when included in a bundle. This initiative is particularly beneficial for students and those establishing a new study or gaming station, as it streamlines the procurement of necessary hardware while lowering the overall cost. Beyond physical hardware, the company is also extending a 10% discount on select Acer Care Plus extended service plans and McAfee LiveSafe antivirus subscriptions, further adding to the value proposition for those looking to protect their new hardware investment.

Expanding Availability of Gaming Hardware

The company’s gaming portfolio remains divided into its high-performance Predator flagship line and the more accessible Nitro series. To maintain a competitive edge, Acer implements rotating weekly deals that apply to a wide range of products, including high-end gaming laptops and monitors. These price adjustments are automatically reflected on the individual product pages, removing the need for customers to manually enter promotional codes during the transaction process.

Performance-minded users often look toward the Nitro series for a balance of power and budget. For example, the Acer Nitro V 16 is currently available with a significant reduction, featuring an Nvidia RTX 5060, 16 GB of RAM, and a one-terabyte storage capacity. This model represents a notable performance tier above the entry-level RTX 5050 version, demonstrating how weekly rotating discounts can provide better hardware specifications for a marginal increase in price. Potential buyers are encouraged to consult resources on the best gaming laptops to ensure their chosen configuration aligns with their specific performance requirements.

Display Technology and Visual Fidelity

Visual performance is a critical factor for modern PC users, and Acer has responded by offering frequent price adjustments on its display inventory. The selection ranges from expansive 49-inch ultrawide monitors to standard 27-inch displays tailored for gaming and professional productivity. Currently, the Acer Nitro XV2 is a notable example of these active, time-limited discounts. The 27-inch 1440p display, which boasts a 300 Hz refresh rate, is currently marked down by 44%, placing it at a more accessible price point for consumers.

Because these deals change on a weekly basis, the inventory of discounted monitors is constantly shifting. Consumers searching for a new desktop PC setup or a monitor upgrade should monitor the Acer storefront regularly. For those evaluating different display standards or seeking guidance on display technology, exploring curated lists of high-performance hardware can provide context on how these current discounts compare to the broader market offerings for a quality monitor.

Verification-Based Discounts for Specific Groups

Acer maintains a dedicated commitment to supporting students, active-duty military, veterans, and healthcare professionals through a structured verification system. Eligible individuals can receive a 15% discount on their purchases by verifying their status through platforms such as Student Beans, SheerID, or the company’s specific healthcare discount portal. This program is intended to lower the barrier to entry for essential technology tools used in educational and professional environments.

The discount applies to a broad variety of the company’s hardware, including thin-and-light options like the Swift 14 AI and the Chromebook Plus 514. By providing these specialized savings, the manufacturer aims to ensure that its laptop lineup remains an attractive and viable option for those who require reliable, high-resolution, and efficient mobile computing. As part of a larger strategy to provide value, these group-specific discounts remain a cornerstone of Acer’s consumer outreach, complementing the broader promotional efforts found on their official online store.
