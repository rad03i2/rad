Hyte X50 PC Case Discounted by $50 During Labor Day Sale

The Hyte X50, known for its unique aesthetic and expansive compatibility, is currently available at a significant discount. PC builders have until September 21st, 2026, to take advantage of the current promotion.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, RDWAN Tech
Published: September 20, 2026
Updated: September 20, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • The Hyte X50 PC case is reduced to $99.99, a $50 discount from its standard pricing.
  • The chassis supports a wide range of motherboard sizes, from ITX to E-ATX, and GPUs up to 430mm.
  • A curved glass panel serves as the central design feature, complemented by high-airflow engineering.
  • The sale includes options like the X50 Air, which is discounted to $79.99.

An Aesthetic Departure from Standard Designs

For PC enthusiasts looking to move away from the traditional black box aesthetic, the [Hyte X50](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=1025X1701640&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fhyte.com%2Fstore%2Fx50%2Fcs-hyte-x50g-rr) offers a distinct visual identity. The case is defined by a massive curved glass panel that wraps around the left side and the top of the chassis. According to reporting from [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/997467/hyte-x50-pc-case-deal-sale), this design choice creates an eye-catching appearance that sets it apart in a crowded market of computer enclosures.

Pricing and Promotional Availability

The promotion is currently active on the [company’s site](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=1025X1701640&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fhyte.com%2Fstore%2Fx50%2Fcs-hyte-x50g-ww), marking a $50 price reduction. This brings the cost of the unit down to $99.99. Potential buyers should note that this deal is part of an ongoing Labor Day sale, which is scheduled to conclude on September 21st, 2026. Hardware Compatibility and Thermal Performance

Despite its focus on aesthetics, the chassis is built to accommodate high-end hardware. It supports a variety of motherboard form factors, spanning from ITX up to E-ATX. Graphics card support is equally robust, with internal clearance for GPUs measuring up to 430mm in length, ensuring that even the most substantial modern cards can fit comfortably. The case is designed to facilitate easy building and provides extensive support for various cooling configurations, including multiple radiator and fan mounting positions.

To address the potential thermal limitations inherent in large glass-panel designs, the manufacturer has integrated specific airflow solutions. The back of the case features louvered fins to assist with heat dissipation, while a porous mesh front facilitates intake. The overall construction is intended to be both refined and functional, catering to builders who prioritize thermal efficiency alongside visual style.

Color Options and Alternative Models

The X50 is available in several bold color schemes to suit different desk setups. Among the choices are 'taro milk,' a lavender-adjacent tone, and 'matcha,' a trending green shade. Traditionalists can still select the classic black or white finishes. For users who prefer a more understated look or enhanced airflow over the glass aesthetics, [Hyte](https://go.skimresources.com/?id=1025X1701640&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fhyte.com%2Fstore%2Fx50%2Fcs-hyte-x50a-ww) also offers the X50 Air. This version replaces the primary glass panel with curved mesh and is currently discounted to $79.99, down from its typical $119.99 price point.
