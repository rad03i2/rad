CyberPowerPC SLC8500WST Packs Ryzen 7 9800X3D and RTX 5080 FE for $2,299

Walmart is offering a CyberPowerPC SLC8500WST gaming desktop equipped with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D and GeForce RTX 5080 Founders Edition for $2,299, undercutting the combined retail price of those two components alone.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 22, 2026
Updated: September 22, 2026 · 5 min read

Key points

  • Prebuilt includes Ryzen 7 9800X3D, RTX 5080 Founders Edition, 32GB DDR5-6000, and 1TB PCIe 4.0 SSD
  • CPU and GPU retail prices total approximately $2,028, leaving $271 for remaining components and assembly
  • RTX 5080 Founders Edition is a scarce reference design with DLSS 5 support
  • Deal fluctuates in stock; Intel Core Ultra 9 285K variant available for $2,399

Deal Overview

Walmart has listed the CyberPowerPC SLC8500WST at $2,299, a configuration that pairs AMD's Ryzen 7 9800X3D processor with Nvidia's GeForce RTX 5080 Founders Edition graphics card. The system also includes 32GB of DDR5-6000 memory and a 1TB PCIe 4.0 SSD. According to Tom's Hardware, the combined retail pricing of the CPU and GPU alone reaches roughly $2,028, meaning the memory, storage, chassis, power supply, and assembly are effectively covered by the remaining $271.

The listing has been moving in and out of stock, prompting the publication to note an alternative configuration featuring an Intel Core Ultra 9 285K paired with the same RTX 5080 Founders Edition for $2,399. Both models are sold under the CyberPowerPC brand and represent aggressive pricing in a market where high-end graphics cards frequently sell above their launch MSRP.

Ryzen 7 9800X3D Remains Gaming Benchmark

The Ryzen 7 9800X3D is an eight-core Zen 5 processor with 96MB of L3 cache, a design that has held the title of fastest gaming CPU for two years since its release. Despite subsequent launches from both AMD and Intel, the 9800X3D continues to lead in gaming performance per dollar. Its 3D V-Cache architecture delivers consistently high frame rates across a wide range of titles, making it the default recommendation for enthusiasts building or buying a gaming-focused system.

In the SLC8500WST, the processor is not throttled by thermal or power constraints typical of compact prebuilt chassis. CyberPowerPC's full-size tower provides adequate airflow for the 120W TDP chip, allowing it to sustain boost clocks during extended gaming sessions. This is a notable advantage over some boutique builds that pair high-end silicon with undersized cooling solutions.

RTX 5080 Founders Edition Brings DLSS 5 and Reference Design

Nvidia's GeForce RTX 5080 Founders Edition is the company's own reference board, distinguished by its dual-flow-through cooler and compact PCB. Unlike partner cards that often carry significant price premiums, the Founders Edition typically launches at MSRP, though availability has been extremely limited. The SLC8500WST secures one of these scarce units, delivering 16GB of GDDR7 memory and full support for DLSS 5, Nvidia's latest AI-driven upscaling and frame generation technology.

Testing by Tom's Hardware has confirmed solid DLSS 5 performance on the RTX 5080, enabling playable 4K frame rates in demanding titles with ray tracing enabled. The Founders Edition's thermal design maintains low noise levels under load, a benefit for users who prioritize acoustics alongside raw performance. Its inclusion in a prebuilt at this price point is unusual, as system integrators more commonly source custom partner models with higher bill-of-materials costs.

Memory and Storage Specifications

The system ships with 32GB of DDR5-6000 memory in a dual-channel 2x16GB configuration. While the Walmart product page does not disclose the exact kit model or latency, the publication notes that the cheapest comparable 32GB DDR5-6000 C30 kit currently retails for approximately $429. At that pricing, the memory alone would consume a significant portion of the $271 margin left after accounting for the CPU and GPU.

Storage consists of a 1TB PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD. Again, the specific controller and NAND type are not listed, but budget 1TB PCIe 4.0 drives start around $149 in the current market, which has been affected by AI-driven demand for high-capacity flash. The capacity is adequate for a modern game library, though power users may wish to add a secondary drive for media or archival storage.

Value Analysis and Market Context

Component-level pricing suggests the SLC8500WST is being sold at or near cost for the core silicon. The Ryzen 7 9800X3D carries a $449 MSRP, while the RTX 5080 Founders Edition starts at $1,579. Adding a conservative $429 for memory and $149 for storage brings the parts subtotal to $2,606 before factoring in the motherboard, power supply, chassis, cooling, Windows license, and labor. This indicates Walmart or CyberPowerPC is absorbing a substantial loss leader to move inventory or acquire customers.

Such pricing is atypical for prebuilt systems, which often pair a flagship GPU with a mid-range CPU, slower memory, or a lower-tier power supply to protect margins. The SLC8500WST avoids those compromises, delivering a balanced high-end specification sheet. For buyers who have delayed upgrades due to inflated GPU pricing, this configuration represents a rare opportunity to acquire a 4K-capable system without navigating the component lottery.

Availability and Purchase Considerations

The deal has shown volatile stock behavior, with the product page alternating between available and sold-out states. Interested buyers are advised to act quickly if the listing appears active. The Intel-based alternative, the SLC8600WST with Core Ultra 9 285K, remains listed at $2,399 and may serve as a fallback for those who prefer Intel's latest Arrow Lake architecture, though it carries a $100 premium.

Warranty and support are handled through CyberPowerPC's standard three-year labor and one-year parts coverage. The system ships with Windows 11 pre-installed. As with any prebuilt, users should verify the motherboard chipset, power supply wattage, and cooling specifics upon receipt, as these details are not exhaustively documented on the retailer page.
