Valve Launches Steam Frame VR Headset Starting at $1,059

After missing its initial early 2026 release window, Valve’s new VR hardware has officially arrived. The device aims to bridge the gap between VR and traditional gaming.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, RDWAN Tech
Published: September 21, 2026
Updated: September 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • Valve’s Steam Frame VR headset is now available with a starting price of $1,059.
  • Nintendo has released Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave, a major new entry in its strategy RPG series.
  • Fujifilm introduced the Instax Pal 2, a compact 10-megapixel digital camera priced at $169.95.
  • The Boox Picco e-reader is now available for preorder, featuring a 3.97-inch E Ink display for $99.99.

Valve Enters the VR Market with Steam Frame

Valve has officially entered the market with the Steam Frame, a new virtual reality headset that finally launched after an expected early 2026 debut. The hardware, which starts at $1,059, is designed to support both immersive VR experiences and standard flatscreen gaming. Early assessments suggest that while the device offers a compelling way to enjoy a wide variety of titles, users should be prepared for potential software bugs and technical instability as Valve works to refine the platform, much like the company did following the initial launch of the Steam Deck.

Nintendo Expands its Strategy RPG Library

In the world of gaming software, Nintendo has released Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave. This latest installment in the long-running strategy RPG series has been described as a significant evolution of the gameplay mechanics introduced in the acclaimed Three Houses. The title is designed as a substantial experience, with some players logging up to 130 hours of gameplay to see all the content available within the Switch 2 ecosystem.

Compact Hardware and Display Innovations

The hardware market continues to see a push toward miniaturization and specialized displays. Fujifilm has debuted the Instax Pal 2, a digital camera retailing for $169.95. The device is notably portable and equipped with an optical viewfinder, a 1.3-inch LCD screen, and a 10-megapixel sensor. Complementing the trend of compact devices, Boox has opened preorders for the Picco, an E Ink e-reader featuring a 3.97-inch screen at a $99.99 price point.

Multimedia and Home Entertainment Updates

Beyond portable gaming and photography, the home entertainment sector is seeing new hardware entries such as Hisense’s RoamView. This mobile 32-inch 4K touchscreen display features a 10-hour battery and is designed to be detached from its wheeled stand for desk use. Meanwhile, cinematic adaptations of popular franchises continue to make waves, with Zach Cregger’s perspective on the Resident Evil series garnering attention for its blend of dark comedy and high-stakes horror elements.

Independent Development and Community Picks

The independent game development scene remains active, highlighted by the work of developer Dan DiIorio, known as TrampolineTales. His recent release, What’s the Password?, offers a focused puzzle experience available across PC and mobile platforms. The wider technology community continues to engage with these developments, frequently sharing recommendations through digital newsletters and forums to stay informed on the best emerging tech and media.
