Major AI Chatbot Apps Now Compatible with Apple CarPlay

Drivers looking for alternatives to Siri while on the road can now access a variety of popular AI chatbot applications directly through the Apple CarPlay interface.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, RDWAN Tech
Published: September 21, 2026
Updated: September 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • An API introduced in iOS 26.4 allows third-party AI chatbots to integrate with Apple CarPlay.
  • Popular apps including ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Meta AI, and Grok are now supported.
  • Chatbots operate exclusively via voice commands for safety while driving.
  • Limitations include a lack of vehicle control, no navigation integration, and the requirement for an active internet connection.

New AI Integration for CarPlay

For drivers who frequently use AI assistants, accessing conversational tools while behind the wheel has become significantly easier. An API introduced with the release of iOS 26.4 has opened the door for developers to bring chatbot capabilities into the vehicle environment. As reported by [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2260401/major-chatbot-apps-work-with-carplay/), this update allows users to interact with various AI services through the CarPlay interface, provided they have installed the latest versions of their preferred applications.

Compatible Chatbot Services

A variety of major AI providers have already adapted their services for in-car use. As noted in [ChatGPT is CarPlay-ready](https://www.engadget.com/ai/openai-brings-chatgpts-voice-mode-to-carplay-191422297.html), users can now utilize OpenAI’s flagship tool by connecting their iPhone and selecting the app icon on the CarPlay Home Screen. Beyond OpenAI, the ecosystem includes [Perplexity](https://apps.apple.com/app/perplexity-ai-search-chat/id1668000334) for search-based queries, as well as [Meta AI](https://apps.apple.com/app/meta-ai/id1558240027) and [xAI's Grok](https://apps.apple.com/app/grok-ai/id6670324846). Most recently, [Anthropic's Claude](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/claude-by-anthropic/id6473753684) has also been added to the list of available services, ensuring that drivers have a wide selection of AI companions for their commutes.

Setup and Safety Requirements

To enable these features, users must first ensure their iPhone is running iOS 26.4 or later by checking the Software Update section in settings. It is also necessary to visit the App Store to ensure that all chatbot applications are updated to their latest versions. Safety remains a priority, and as such, these AI tools operate exclusively through voice commands to keep the driver's attention on the road. Users can initiate a new voice chat or resume existing conversations that originated on other devices.

For those using the latest iPhone 15 Pro or newer, Apple’s own Siri AI is also available if the device is updated to iOS 27. Unlike third-party chatbots, Siri AI features integration with Apple Maps, making it the only option currently capable of providing navigation assistance.

Limitations and Performance Considerations

While these integrations offer new functionality, they come with notable limitations. Currently, users cannot trigger these bots using a wake word, meaning the app must be opened manually within the CarPlay interface. Furthermore, these chatbots cannot control vehicle hardware or interact with other applications. Performance is also heavily dependent on the quality of the car's built-in microphones and the strength of the user's internet connection. If a connection is weak or lost, the service will cease to function.

It is also important to note that these tools do not have access to real-time location data for navigation purposes. Users can ask for general recommendations or entertainment, but they should remain aware that AI responses are not always accurate and should be verified. Due to potential distractions, users are encouraged to customize their CarPlay home screen by rearranging icons to ensure that the most important tools are easily accessible.
