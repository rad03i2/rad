Apple Launches State-of-the-Art Apple Music Hall in London

The technology giant has unveiled a new high-tech performance space in the historic Battersea Power Station, aiming to bridge the gap between intimate live shows and professional production capabilities.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 22, 2026
Updated: September 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • Apple Music Hall is a 600-capacity venue located in London's storied Battersea Power Station.
  • The space is equipped with a 48-speaker spatial sound system and integrated professional recording studios.
  • Artists can produce high-quality multicamera video and spatial audio mixes directly from the venue.
  • London-based Apple Music Radio shows have relocated their operations to new studios at the same location.

A New Chapter for Live Music in London

Apple has officially expanded its footprint in the music industry with the opening of Apple Music Hall, a sophisticated live performance venue situated within the historic Battersea Power Station in London. According to the official [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-opens-apple-music-hall-a-state-of-the-art-live-music-venue-in-london/) announcement, the facility is designed to offer a unique experience that blends the intimacy of a club setting with the high-end production infrastructure typically reserved for large-scale arena tours.

Oliver Schusser, Apple’s vice president of Apple Music and International Content, noted that the project represents an unprecedented step in the company's commitment to artists and fans. By leveraging its history in the music space—stretching from the debut of the iPod to the modern streaming era—Apple aims to provide creators with specialized tools that were previously inaccessible in a small-venue environment.

Architectural Design and Technical Capabilities

The design of the venue pays homage to its iconic location, incorporating the Battersea Power Station’s heritage brickwork, fluted arches, and bronze balconies. A striking feature of the space is a large glass wall that provides views of the river and the surrounding concourse. With a capacity of 600, the hall is structured to ensure that every attendee remains close to the performer while benefiting from a highly adaptable 38-foot-wide stage.

Technologically, the venue is built for the future of audio. It features a 48-speaker spatial sound system arranged throughout the audience area, allowing for an immersive listening environment. This infrastructure supports the company's broader push into immersive audio found at [apple.com/apple-music](https://www.apple.com/apple-music/).

Integrated Production and Broadcast Features

Beyond its utility as a concert space, Apple Music Hall functions as a full-scale professional production facility. The venue is equipped with two dedicated recording and mix studios that meet professional standards. Artists can record multitrack performances and create mixes in Spatial Audio in real-time or as a post-production process.

The facility is also hardwired for multicamera capture, supporting 16 or more cameras alongside iPhone-based video capture. This setup feeds into a purpose-built broadcast control room, enabling artists to walk away from a single performance with a finished, broadcast-ready product. Performances held at the venue will also be available for global livestreaming, ensuring that fans who cannot attend in person can view these exclusive shows via [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com/).

Centralizing Apple Music Radio Operations

In addition to the concert hall, the company announced the relocation of its Apple Music Radio operations in London to new studios within the Battersea complex. This move centralizes the streaming service's radio production, which is known for its artist-led interviews, global premieres, and 24/7 curated content.

Zane Lowe, Apple Music’s global creative director and lead anchor for Apple Music 1, expressed enthusiasm for the new space, emphasizing its role in fostering deeper connections between artists and fans. The facility serves as a destination where creators and listeners can interact in a shared, professional environment.

Access and Future Updates

Apple has established several digital channels for fans to keep up with developments at the new venue. Detailed information regarding ticketing for upcoming events can be found at [applemusichall.com](https://www.applemusichall.com/). Additionally, the company will share ongoing updates and content via official social media channels, including their [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/applemusichall) and [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@applemusichall) accounts.
