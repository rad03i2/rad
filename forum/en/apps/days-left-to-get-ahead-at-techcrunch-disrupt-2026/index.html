TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 Ticket Price Hike Imminent

Founders, investors, and tech leaders have less than one week to secure early-bird pricing for the upcoming innovation summit in San Francisco.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, RDWAN Tech
Published: September 21, 2026
Updated: September 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • Current ticket savings of up to $200 remain available until September 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT.
  • The event takes place October 13–15 in San Francisco, featuring over 10,000 attendees.
  • The conference agenda includes 200+ sessions and 250+ speakers across six industry stages.
  • Group registration for four or more participants offers an additional 30% discount.

Countdown to Disrupt 2026

As the technology landscape continues to evolve, industry professionals are preparing for TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, scheduled to take place in San Francisco from October 13 to October 15. Organizers have announced that the current window to save up to $200 on registration fees is closing fast. Prospective attendees must complete their registration by September 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT to lock in current pricing before rates increase.

What to Expect at the Summit

The event serves as a hub for more than 10,000 founders, investors, operators, and tech leaders to collaborate and discuss the future of the startup ecosystem. According to the event organizers, TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 will facilitate deeper engagement through a combination of structured content and informal networking, proving that the value isn’t just onstage.

The extensive programming includes 200+ sessions led by 250+ speakers. These presentations are spread across six industry stages, offering insights into critical sectors such as artificial intelligence, robotics, fintech, infrastructure, and the future of work. Participants are encouraged to register now to get up to $200 in ticket savings and access these expert-led discussions.

Networking and Startup Discovery

Beyond the keynote stages and educational sessions, the conference aims to foster high-value connections. Attendees will have access to AI-powered matchmaking services, ad hoc meeting spaces, and the Deal Flow Cafe, which is specifically designed for those holding an investor pass or a founder pass. These resources are intended to help participants identify potential partners, clients, or future employees.

The Expo Hall will host more than 300 startups, allowing attendees to preview emerging technologies before they hit the mainstream market. Furthermore, the conference will host the Startup Battlefield 200, an intense pitch competition that highlights promising new ventures. These elements are designed to ensure that participants leave the conference with actionable insights, such as refined fundraising strategies and go-to-market tactics.

Registration Deadlines

In addition to general attendee passes, the event offers group discounts for teams of four or more, providing an additional 30% reduction in price. As the September 25 deadline approaches, organizers have emphasized that the opportunity to save money on tickets is limited. Get your ticket by Sept. 25 at 11:59 p.m. PT and save up to $200 before prices rise.
