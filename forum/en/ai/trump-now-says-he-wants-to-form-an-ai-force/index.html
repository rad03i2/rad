Trump Proposes New 'AI Force' and Appointment of AI Czar

President Donald Trump has announced plans to establish a new federal 'AI Force' overseen by an appointed 'AI czar.' The move comes as the administration positions itself to champion, rather than restrict, the rapidly evolving artificial intelligence sector.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, RDWAN Tech
Published: September 20, 2026
Updated: September 20, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • President Trump announced plans for an 'AI Force' and the appointment of an 'AI czar' via Truth Social.
  • The administration claims it intends to foster industry growth without hindering development.
  • The proposal arrives amidst rising political and industry pressure to slow down AI advancement.
  • The president compared the initiative to the Space Force and made claims regarding the economic benefits of data centers.

An Ambitious Federal Initiative

President Trump recently revealed his intention to create a new federal entity dubbed the 'AI Force,' an announcement [made](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-says-he-will-create-ai-force-name-ai-czar-2026-09-19/) via a post on Truth Social. Central to this plan is the appointment of an 'AI czar,' an official who would be tasked with leading the force and overseeing the nation’s strategic direction regarding artificial intelligence.

This [announcement](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqlykr2vrv04o) arrives at a critical juncture in the tech policy landscape. As the capabilities of generative AI continue to expand, there have been [growing calls](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996923/ai-safety-slow-openai-anthropic) from both political figures and industry leaders to implement stricter safety measures. Many experts and executives have urged policymakers to [pump the brakes](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994337/anthropic-ceo-slow-down-ai-development) on rapid AI development to ensure that safety and ethical standards are properly established before systems become too advanced.

Administration Stance on AI Growth

In his statement, President Trump clarified that his administration does not intend to impede the progress of the technology sector. He emphasized that the government will 'not in any way hinder or stifle the Growth of this incredible Industry.' Instead, he suggested that his administration aims to 'cherish it, help it, and watch over it, as it grows.'

Economic Claims and Infrastructure

A significant portion of the president's message focused on the role of data centers in local communities. Trump described these facilities as 'wealthy and prestigious' and asserted that they provide tangible benefits to their host regions. According to the president, the construction of data centers leads to higher salaries, lower taxes, and safer streets. However, these claims have been met with skepticism by analysts, as the president provided no specific evidence to support the assertion that these infrastructure projects inherently improve community welfare.

Future Leadership and Oversight

While the proposal for an 'AI Force' echoes the organizational model of the Space Force—which Trump categorized as a 'tremendous SUCCESS'—the administration has yet to release a timeline for the project. Specific details regarding the scope of the new agency's authority or its operational budget remain unclear.

Regarding the potential leadership of this new force, the president noted that the position of AI czar will be highly exclusive. In his post, Trump insisted that 'Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!' for the role, though no specific candidates or vetting processes have been identified as of late September 2026.
