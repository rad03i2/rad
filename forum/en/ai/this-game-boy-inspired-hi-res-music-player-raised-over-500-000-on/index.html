Game Boy-Inspired AP30 Music Boy Raises Over $500,000

The AP30 Music Boy has successfully concluded its crowdfunding campaign, raising over $500,000 as it taps into the rising demand for dedicated, distraction-free audio hardware.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, RDWAN Tech
Published: September 21, 2026
Updated: September 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • The AP30 Music Boy campaign raised over $500,000, significantly exceeding its original goal.
  • The device features a retro Game Boy aesthetic while offering high-resolution audio support.
  • It doubles as a portable DAC and includes additional functionality like e-book reading and retro gaming.
  • The project is part of a wider movement toward 'dumb' tech that encourages unplugging from smartphone distractions.

A Resurgence in Minimalist Hardware

The technology sector is currently witnessing a notable shift as consumers move toward minimalist devices that prioritize offline capability. This trend is evident in the popularity of [flip phones that reject social media](https://www.engadget.com/2195276/commodore-made-a-social-media-banishing-flip-phone/) and a general desire to reclaim focus from constant digital connectivity. HIDIZS has captured this sentiment with the AP30 Music Boy, a portable high-resolution audio player that mirrors the nostalgic design of a vintage handheld gaming console.

The [AP30 Music Boy's Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/hidizs/ap30-music-boy-the-first-truly-wearable-hi-res-music-player) was initiated with a modest target of HK$40,000, or approximately $5,100. By the time the campaign concluded, it had generated over $504,311, representing a figure more than 98 times its initial goal. While the primary campaign has closed, the manufacturer has indicated that late backers may still participate in the project.

Technical Specifications and Design

Designed for audiophiles who value portability, the Music Boy is available in two variants: an 80g aluminum model and a 56g plastic version. To accommodate different lifestyles, the device offers three distinct carrying options, including a magnetic attachment for smartphones, a lanyard, and a bag clip. Although the unit lacks internal memory, it supports microSD cards of up to 256GB, providing ample storage for high-fidelity audio files.

The player is compatible with a wide array of file formats, including MP3, WAV, APE, FLAC, ALAC, OGG, WMA, DSF, and DFF. It supports PCM playback up to 192kHz/24-bit and native DSD256. For those seeking a modern integration, the device can connect to computers or smartphones via USB-C to function as a portable digital-to-analog converter (DAC). Battery performance is rated at 1200mAh, offering between eight to 10 hours of wired playback or six to eight hours of wireless usage.

Beyond Music Playback

The AP30 Music Boy aims to offer more than just audio playback. It incorporates physical controls on the front panel, supplemented by a side-mounted scroll wheel for navigation. The device also features six equalization presets, with options for user-defined customization. Beyond music, the hardware includes e-book reading capabilities and six retro-style games, such as Snake and a title reminiscent of Tetris, reinforcing its identity alongside other [aesthetic-focused music players](https://www.engadget.com/tom-vek-sleevenote-090019190.html).

The Context of Offline Tech

The success of this product arrives as [searches for vintage iPods have soared](https://www.engadget.com/2196894/ipods-comeback-gen-z/), reflecting a generational desire to untether listening experiences from the smartphone. For many, the integration of music services into notification-heavy mobile devices has compromised the focused enjoyment of audio. The Music Boy project posits that while smartphones modernized music consumption, they also diminished the 'magic' of the dedicated listening experience.

This trend is particularly prevalent among Gen-Z, a demographic currently demonstrating a strong affinity for Y2K fashion and early internet aesthetics. By moving away from invasive algorithms and constant online connectivity, users are seeking the mental relief associated with simpler, singular-purpose technology. Even as AI-driven features become increasingly embedded in modern hardware, the AP30 Music Boy serves as a practical, tangible alternative for those looking to unplug.
