New Mac mini and Mac Studio Launch With M6 and M5 Chips

Apple’s latest desktop lineup is now available for purchase, introducing significant hardware upgrades designed for both everyday users and creative professionals.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 22, 2026
Updated: September 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • The new Mac mini is now available with M6 and M5 Pro chips, starting at $899.
  • The Mac Studio arrives with M5 Max and M5 Ultra options, designed for high-end professional workloads.
  • New hardware features significant gains in AI performance, graphics, and CPU speed compared to previous generations.
  • Customers can access specialized support, trade-in programs, and educational discounts starting today.

The Next Generation of Apple Desktop Performance

Starting today, September 22, Apple has officially made its latest desktop computers available to the public. Customers can now shop for [Mac mini](https://www.apple.com/mac-mini/) with M6 and M5 Pro chips, as well as the high-performance [Mac Studio](https://www.apple.com/mac-studio/) equipped with M5 Max and M5 Ultra processors. These machines are accessible through Apple Store locations, [apple.com](https://www.apple.com), and the official Apple Store app.

Versatility and Power in the M6-Powered Mac mini

The latest Mac mini has been positioned as Apple’s most versatile desktop, catering to a diverse audience ranging from students and everyday users to developers and creative enthusiasts. According to Apple, the M6-equipped model delivers up to 40 percent faster CPU performance, 2x faster graphics and storage, and a substantial 4x increase in AI performance. This boost is intended to assist with multitasking, agentic AI workflows, and demanding creative projects such as coding and music production.

For those requiring more intensive processing capabilities, the Mac mini with M5 Pro offers pro-level performance suitable for game development and video production. The base model for the M6 version starts at $899, while the M5 Pro variant begins at $1,699. Students can take advantage of exclusive [education](https://www.apple.com/us-edu/store/) pricing, which lowers the starting cost of the M6 Mac mini to $799.

Mac Studio Targets High-End Workloads

The Mac Studio remains Apple’s most powerful desktop option, now featuring the M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips. This iteration is specifically engineered for professionals handling the most demanding tasks in the industry. Performance metrics indicate up to 4.3x faster AI performance and 1.8x faster graphics compared to predecessor hardware. Additionally, the system supports up to 512GB of unified memory, though availability for this specific high-memory configuration is slated for late October.

Pricing for the Mac Studio starts at $2,499 for the M5 Max model and $5,499 for the M5 Ultra. Eligible customers in the United States can utilize leasing options through Apple Upgrade, with monthly payments starting as low as $48.99 for the M5 Max model on a 36-month term. These devices are designed to maintain a compact footprint while offering massive compute power for professional workflows.

Advancing AI and Software Integration

Both the new Mac mini and Mac Studio are built to serve as platforms for on-device AI. They incorporate next-generation GPUs with Neural Accelerators in every core, supported by advanced CPUs and dedicated Neural Engines. These hardware enhancements are designed to work in tandem with [macOS 27](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/major-updates-for-apples-software-platforms-are-now-available/) to support the latest Apple Intelligence features. A core component of this ecosystem is [Siri AI](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/), which Apple describes as a more capable and personal digital assistant.

Shopping, Setup, and Support Services

Apple has implemented several support initiatives to assist customers with their new purchases. Through [apple.com](https://www.apple.com), users can engage with specialists via chat or video call for personalized shopping advice. For those looking to upgrade from older machines, the [Apple Trade In](https://www.apple.com/shop/trade-in/) program allows users to receive credit based on the value of their current computer.

Once a device is acquired, Apple offers Personal Setup sessions to guide users through data transfers and software features. Additionally, customers can attend free [Today at Apple](https://www.apple.com/today/) sessions at local retail stores to learn new creative and technical skills from Apple Creatives, covering everything from coding to professional music production. Dedicated business teams are also available to assist small- and medium-sized enterprises with bulk purchasing and integration needs.
