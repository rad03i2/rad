OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna Models

OpenAI is expanding its GPT-6 generation with two new additions: the balanced GPT-6 Sol and the lightweight, cost-efficient GPT-6 Luna.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 22, 2026
Updated: September 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna following the earlier launch of GPT-6 Astra.
  • GPT-6 Sol is targeted at interactive and agentic coding, while Luna is built for smaller, faster tasks.
  • The new models feature significant price drops for API access, operating at half the cost of the 5.6 series.
  • The models are rolling out across GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the ChatGPT API.

Expanding the GPT-6 Family

OpenAI has expanded its flagship artificial intelligence lineup by introducing two new models to the GPT-6 generation: GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna. These additions follow the earlier release of GPT-6 Astra, which the company previously introduced as its most powerful model yet for computer work and coding. According to official details from the GitHub Changelog, the new models join the ecosystem to give developers more options that fit specific tasks, ranging from everyday agentic coding to fast, cost-efficient assistance.

The expansion brings targeted capabilities to different user tiers. GPT-6 Sol is positioned as a balanced model designed for interactive and agentic coding, making it a strong all-round choice for development tasks that require careful, multistep validation. Meanwhile, GPT-6 Luna serves as a lightweight, cost-efficient model built for smaller, faster tasks, representing the lowest-cost option in the current GPT-6 family.

Efficiency, Affordability, and Error Reduction

Alongside architectural refinements, OpenAI is emphasizing major improvements in efficiency and affordability. Reporting from TechCrunch highlights that the 6 series models will be accessible via API at half the cost of the preceding 5.6 series, a price drop attributed to improvements in caching and inference. Furthermore, OpenAI claims that its newest models are better at producing factually accurate answers and boast lower error rates in software development.

In internal factuality evaluations based on de-identified real-world conversations, OpenAI states that GPT-6 Sol makes roughly half as many mistakes as its predecessor. The company reports that this achieves Astra-level reliability at a substantially lower cost point. Luna, on the other hand, is aimed at high-volume clerical tasks with clear goals, such as summarizing documents, extracting information, or answering quick questions.

Availability and Platform Integration

The rollout of the new models spans multiple developer and enterprise environments. In GitHub Copilot, GPT-6 Sol is available to Copilot Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise plans, while GPT-6 Luna is available to Copilot Pro, Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise plans. Administrators managing Copilot Business and Enterprise plans can configure access using the model policy settings within Copilot.

Outside of GitHub, TechCrunch notes that the new versions of Sol and Luna are rolling out to ChatGPT Work and Codex for most paid accounts, as well as the ChatGPT API. Luna is also slated for availability within the desktop application and for Free and Go users, with a gradual rollout across the main ChatGPT app and website throughout the day.

Competitive Landscape

The release of GPT-6 Sol and Luna arrives amid an intensely competitive landscape in the artificial intelligence sector. OpenAI's launch closely coincides with rival developments from Anthropic, reflecting a fast-paced environment where leading AI labs frequently update their model lineups to capture market share among developers and enterprise customers.
