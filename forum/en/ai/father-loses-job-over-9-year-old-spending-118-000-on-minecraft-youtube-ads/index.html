Father Loses Job After Son Spends $118,000 on YouTube Ads

A father is facing a massive financial burden after his young son accidentally racked up six-figure charges on a corporate credit card. The incident has resulted in the father’s termination from his job and a frantic search for a solution to the mounting debt.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 21, 2026
Updated: September 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • A 9-year-old Minecraft content creator accidentally spent $118,479.83 on YouTube ad campaigns.
  • The charges were processed on the father’s corporate American Express Business Gold Rewards Card.
  • The father has since lost his employment and faces a 30-day deadline to settle the balance.
  • Despite the severity of the financial situation, the family has refused to launch a GoFundMe or crypto-based fundraiser.

An Unexpected Financial Crisis

A routine attempt to promote a YouTube channel took a catastrophic turn when a 9-year-old Minecraft player known as MightyMikePlays67 ended up spending a massive $118,000 on their dad’s company credit card. The unintended ad campaign, which was originally intended to be a minor promotional effort, ballooned into a six-figure debt within just three weeks. As reported by Tom's Hardware, the father, identified as Dave, provided a detailed account of the events on his YouTube channel, explaining that the situation has led to him being fired from his job.

The Impact of the Unauthorized Spending

The scale of the expenditure was verified by a screenshot of the credit card statement, which displayed a total of $118,479.83 charged to an American Express Business Gold Rewards Card. Facing this daunting figure, the family now faces the prospect of selling their home to satisfy the debt, which must be repaid within a 30-day window. While some observers have questioned the circumstances of the incident, Dave remains focused on the reality of his situation, noting that he is currently managing the fallout without the aid of major financial assistance programs. Further details are available from Terms & Conditions in the original source material.

Refusal of Public Fundraising

In the wake of the public disclosure, many viewers suggested that the father set up a GoFundMe or explore cryptocurrency solutions to generate funds. However, Dave has explicitly rejected these options. He clarified that no official fundraising campaigns exist, stating, “We do not have a Mighty Mike coin. We don’t have a 67 coin. We don’t have any coins. No crypto. We don’t have a GoFundMe. We have no fund raising set up.” Instead of seeking donations, he has attempted to mitigate losses by launching a merchandise website featuring designs inspired by his son’s channel, though availability remains limited to a few specific items.

Moving Forward Under Pressure

The emotional toll of the incident is evident in the father's commentary. Reflecting on his outlook, he joked that his retirement plans have been significantly altered, remarking, “I’m going to be working until I’m like 94.” Despite the pressure, he has maintained a stoic approach toward his job loss, asserting his capability to find new employment. While he has been encouraged by others to approach major news outlets to broadcast his story, he remains skeptical about whether public attention would change his financial reality or provide the relief needed to address the debt.
