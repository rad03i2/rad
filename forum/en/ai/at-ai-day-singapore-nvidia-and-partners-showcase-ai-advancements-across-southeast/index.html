NVIDIA and Partners Highlight AI Advancements at Singapore Event

NVIDIA and its ecosystem partners are gathering at the Raffles City Convention Centre to demonstrate how advanced AI tools and open models are accelerating technological development across Southeast Asia.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 23, 2026
Updated: September 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • NVIDIA AI Day Singapore takes place on September 22-23 at the Raffles City Convention Centre.
  • Governments and enterprises across Southeast Asia are utilizing open models and developer tools to move AI from experimentation to production.
  • Singapore's HTX is exploring NVIDIA Nemotron models to advance public safety applications.
  • Sea Limited has become the first enterprise in the ASEAN region to adopt the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform.

NVIDIA AI Day Singapore Spotlights Regional Innovation

The technology landscape in Southeast Asia is taking center stage as industry leaders gather for NVIDIA AI Day Singapore. Held at the Raffles City Convention Centre, the multi-day event provides attendees with hands-on training, expert-led sessions, and advanced tools designed to accelerate work in artificial intelligence and high-performance computing.

Throughout the conference, NVIDIA and its extensive partner ecosystem are highlighting breakthrough AI advancements spanning the entire region. Organizers emphasize that nations are increasingly treating artificial intelligence as a matter of national strategy, shifting focus from initial experimental pilots to full production-scale deployments.

Advancing Public Sector AI and Citizen Services

Governments across Southeast Asia are building trusted AI capabilities that reflect local languages, cultures, and economic priorities. NVIDIA is supporting this transition by providing necessary infrastructure, developer tools, and open models to enhance public sector operations, citizen services, and critical infrastructure.

In Singapore, the Home Team Science and Technology Agency (HTX) is actively researching how to apply sophisticated machine learning architectures. HTX is embarking on research using the NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super and Nemotron 3 Nano Omni models to strengthen secure, locally controlled public safety applications across the nation's Home Team.

Other regional enterprises are also scaling up their capabilities. NCS is expanding enterprise adoption using Nemotron foundation models and advanced automation frameworks, while ST Engineering utilizes specialized development tools and route optimization software to build its internal AI studio platform.

Regional Collaboration and Localized Language Models

Beyond Singapore, organizations throughout Southeast Asia are fine-tuning open foundation models to serve domestic linguistic and industrial needs. Malaysia's YTL AI Labs is fine-tuning models for local citizen services, while Viettel AI in Vietnam is optimizing advanced architectures for Vietnamese-language applications and legal tech implementations.

In Thailand, members of the ThaiLLM Collaboration are exploring foundation model architectures for specialized use cases. As part of this cooperative push, local technology providers are adapting nano-scale variants by fine-tuning open legal datasets with specialized regional frameworks, driving widespread adoption in legal-assistant chatbots.

Scaling Infrastructure Across ASEAN

Infrastructure development remains a core pillar of the region's technological expansion. Companies are leveraging computer vision models and intelligent search blueprints to optimize urban environments, from managing traffic operations in Malaysia to improving passenger flow across major airports in Thailand.

In a major milestone for enterprise computing infrastructure in the region, Sea Limited has made headlines by becoming the first enterprise in the ASEAN region to adopt the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform. This deployment significantly scales the global technology company's computational capabilities, allowing it to better serve communities and expand digital economic opportunities throughout Southeast Asia.
