F-Prime Capital to Present Robotics Investment Report at RoboBusiness

Analyzing the Flow of Robotics Capital

As the global demand for automation continues to accelerate, the financial health of the robotics sector remains a focal point for investors and developers alike. With the industry having raised a substantial $4.87 billion across 162 financial transactions in August 2026 alone, the need for clear market analysis has never been greater. For those tracking this rapid [funding] landscape, the upcoming unveiling of the annual State of Robotics Investments report provides a critical look at where capital is flowing and what it means for the future of the industry.

Expert Insights at RoboBusiness 2026

The report will be presented by Sanjay Aggarwal and Betsy Mulé, both of whom represent [F-Prime Capital]. Their presentation is scheduled to take place at [RoboBusiness], which is set to convene on October 20 and 21 in Santa Clara, California. Attendees of the session can expect a comprehensive examination of the latest trends in robotics, including details on which specific sectors are currently capturing the most investor attention and which emerging technologies are gaining traction in the marketplace.

The presentation is designed to provide more than just raw data; it aims to offer actionable insights. Investors and robotics company founders alike will be able to gauge the requirements for financing successful ventures in the current economic climate, helping them navigate the competitive landscape of modern enterprise technology.

Meet the Presenters

Sanjay Aggarwal brings a wealth of experience to the discussion as a partner at F-Prime Capital, focusing specifically on frontier technology and enterprise software. His career includes a tenure as CEO of Unicel Technologies, a firm he scaled and successfully exited to Karix Mobile. His background also includes roles at Devonshire Investors and McKinsey and Co., alongside technical experience gained at Berkeley Process Control. Aggarwal holds degrees from MIT and UC Berkeley.

Betsy Mulé, a principal at the firm, specializes in early-stage investments within the enterprise technology and robotics spheres. Prior to her time at the investment firm, she served as a strategy consultant at Mars & Co., advising numerous Fortune 500 organizations across Europe, Latin America, and the United States. Mulé is an alumna of MIT, holding a degree in chemical-biological engineering.
