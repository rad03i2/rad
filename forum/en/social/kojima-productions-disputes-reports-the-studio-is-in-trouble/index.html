Kojima Productions Rebuts Financial Difficulty Rumors

Kojima Productions has issued a formal statement countering recent speculation about the studio's stability. The announcement follows reports that Sony dropped the upcoming project, Physint.

Radwan AbdulhadiFounder and editor, Mikhbar
Published: September 21, 2026
Updated: September 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key points

  • Kojima Productions confirmed it remains in a healthy and profitable state despite recent industry rumors.
  • The studio stated its relationship with PlayStation remains strong following the shift of its latest project.
  • Hideo Kojima addressed industry-wide concerns regarding studio closures and project cancellations.
  • Reports from Bloomberg had previously suggested the departure from Sony was driven by budgetary and profitability concerns.

Addressing Industry Speculation

Kojima Productions has officially moved to clarify its current financial standing in the wake of widespread industry speculation. Following the announcement that Sony would no longer be involved with [Physint](https://www.theverge.com/games/993205/physint-hideo-kojima-sony-playstation-microsoft-xbox), the upcoming title from the famed creator of Metal Gear Solid, rumors began to circulate regarding the studio’s viability and internal stability. Reports suggested that the project's transition to Xbox was the result of a significant falling out between the developer and Sony.

In a formal statement released via social media, the studio explicitly addressed these claims, asserting that it is currently in a healthy, profitable state. This public outreach was designed to counteract a narrative fueled by reports, such as those from [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/hideo-kojima-s-physint-video-game-dropped-by-playstation-goes-to-xbox), which suggested the project's cancellation at Sony was due to missed deadlines, concerns over profitability, and budgetary pressures.

Contextualizing the Studio's Future

The atmosphere surrounding the studio had become increasingly tense following news of the project's move to Xbox. In an exclusive [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/hideo-kojima-reveals-what-happened-with-sony-in-exclusive-first-interview-since-playstation-ditched-physint) interview, Hideo Kojima reflected on the broader difficulties currently facing the gaming sector. Kojima acknowledged the volatility of the current market, noting that numerous studios have faced closures and multiple titles have been subject to sudden cancellations across the industry.

During this candid discussion, Kojima admitted that he felt an initial sense of surprise when the situation regarding his latest project began to unfold. He noted his awareness of the vulnerability of development teams when contracts are terminated or funding is withdrawn. He specifically addressed the fear that the loss of a major contract could force a studio to consider layoffs or even face the prospect of total collapse due to depleted cash flow.

Clarifying the Relationship with PlayStation

Beyond the financial reassurances, Kojima Productions sought to stabilize the narrative surrounding its business partnerships. Despite the shift of the new title to Xbox, the studio emphasized that its relationship with PlayStation remains strong. This statement aims to mitigate concerns that the departure from the Sony ecosystem signaled a total breakdown in communication or professional cooperation between the two entities.

The studio further addressed rumors that suggested disappointing sales performance regarding its previous works—specifically Death Stranding and its successor—were contributing factors to the current financial narrative. By categorizing itself as a healthy and profitable entity, the developer is actively distancing itself from the pessimistic outlook currently permeating discussions about its operational future.

Market Resilience and Continued Development

As the [Gaming](https://www.theverge.com/games) landscape continues to shift, developers are finding themselves under increasing scrutiny regarding project budgets and timelines. Kojima Productions appears determined to maintain its creative independence while managing the external pressures that come with high-profile game development. The studio's commitment to transparently countering rumors marks a significant step in preserving its reputation among both industry stakeholders and its player base.

With the clarification provided in its recent social media statement, the focus now returns to the studio's output. The industry is currently watching to see how the transition of its latest major project to a new publisher will affect development schedules and final product quality. For now, the studio remains focused on its internal operations, aiming to move past the speculative headlines that characterized the transition.
