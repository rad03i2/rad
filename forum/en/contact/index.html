Editorial feedback

Use the public Mikhbar issue form on GitHub for editorial feedback, questions or coverage suggestions. Include the relevant Mikhbar URL whenever possible.

Correction requests

For a correction, first review the corrections policy, then provide the article URL, the information you believe needs review and a supporting source when available.

The editorial policy explains how Mikhbar handles sources, headlines, updates and the distinction between reporting and analysis.
